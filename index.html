A personal assistant you can call.

Velqor answers by phone, Telegram, WhatsApp and a web dashboard, with one memory behind all of them. It reads your mail and calendar, keeps your tasks, and asks before it sends anything.

What it does today

Version 0.1 runs on your own server with your own keys.

Phone calls

Ring your Velqor number and talk. Replies are spoken as they are written and you can interrupt mid-sentence. It can phone you too, for a reminder or the morning brief.

Telegram and WhatsApp

Send text, photos, PDFs or voice notes. On Telegram, anything that needs your sign-off arrives with Approve and Decline buttons.

A dashboard that listens

Works on a phone and a laptop. The core at its centre reacts to your voice and to what Velqor is doing, and the transcript shows every channel in one place.

Email and calendar

Searches and reads Gmail, drafts replies, checks your schedule and books events. Sending and booking wait for your yes.

Memory, tasks and reminders

Remembers what you tell it and shows you every saved fact with a Forget button beside it. Keeps a task list and reminds you by message or by call.

It speaks first when it should

A morning brief of your calendar, mail and tasks. An evening nudge for anything due. Reminders at the time you set.

Photos and documents

Send a receipt, a whiteboard or a contract. Velqor reads it and acts on what it finds.

Other callers get a receptionist

Anyone else who rings hears an AI assistant that takes a message and passes it on. It tells them nothing about you.

It asks before it acts

An assistant with access to your mail needs rules it cannot talk its way around. Every action belongs to one of three tiers.

Runs at onceReading mail and calendar, searching the web, your own notes, tasks and reminders.
Waits for your yesSending email, creating or deleting calendar events. You see the exact recipient, subject and body first.
Needs a codeAnything that spends money. You repeat a one-time code. No payment tools ship yet, but the tier is built and tested.
  • The model cannot approve itself. Approvals are matched in plain code against your own words or button presses, so text hidden in an email cannot approve anything.
  • Every door is checked. Twilio requests are signature-verified, Telegram requests carry a shared secret, and each call gets a single-use ticket.
  • A PIN for the phone. Caller ID can be faked, so phone instructions can be locked behind a spoken or keyed PIN.
  • No card or ID numbers in memory. They are rejected at the point of saving.

Roadmap

Seven phases, built in order. The first three are in the repository now.

  1. Core

    Agent brain, memory, text channels, email, calendar and approval tiers.

    Built

  2. Voice

    Inbound and outbound phone calls with streamed, interruptible speech.

    Built

  3. Proactive and visual

    Morning brief, nudges, reminders, photo and file handling, and the dashboard.

    Built

  4. Media

    Music playback across devices, a laptop helper app, and TV control.

    Next

  5. Commerce

    Shopping and food ordering, with a spending cap and a code for every purchase.

    Planned

  6. Wearables

    A watch app, hands-free voice through smart glasses, and sleep and activity data from a ring.

    Planned

  7. Beyond one person

    Calls to businesses on your behalf, multiple user accounts, and home automation.

    Later

Where it stands

Version 0.1 is built. Its 26 automated tests pass and the dashboard has been exercised end to end. It has not yet been run against live Anthropic, Twilio, Telegram or Google accounts, which is the next step. The source code is in a private repository while that testing happens.
